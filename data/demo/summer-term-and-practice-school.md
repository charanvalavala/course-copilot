# Summer term and Practice School dates

The 2025-26 calendar lists May 21, 2026 as the start of summer vacation and the summer term. Practice School I begins May 25, 2026. Practice School II ends June 19, 2026, and Practice School I ends July 18, 2026. Refer to the calendar for programme-specific variations.
