# How a course gets offered

The BITS Pilani Academic Bulletin 2025-26 says course offerings depend on programme progression, facilities, and the decision of the Dean of AUGSD or AGSRD. The term timetable lists courses actually offered and is published at the beginning of the semester. A bulletin listing alone does not confirm that the course will run.
